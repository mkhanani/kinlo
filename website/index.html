An assistant that asks first

Ask about your people, or tell it what happened in a sentence. It looks things up by itself. Every change is shown to you before anything is saved.

How the assistant works

Find anyone in a keystroke

Ctrl K, or Cmd K on a Mac, searches names, companies, notes and everything said in past conversations.

Import people from Google Contacts, Outlook or any spreadsheet. Nobody already in kinlo is changed.
